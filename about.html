About Austin Marshall

Hello! My name is Austin Marshall, and I am an engineering student at Arizona State University with a strong passion for design, welding, and digital fabrication. I apply my hands-on experience and education to my role at our family-owned manufacturing shop, Halo Structural Products. We specialize in crafting high-quality custom anchor bolts for large construction projects and providing precision CNC plasma-cutting services for specialized steel plates. Whether you are a commercial contractor looking for reliable structural hardware or a local maker needing custom metal parts, our shop combines traditional hard work with modern technology to deliver exactly what you need.
