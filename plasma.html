CNC Plasma Cutting Services

The Hypertherm XPR300

At Halo Structural Products, we use a top-of-the-line Hypertherm XPR300 plasma table. This machine is incredibly powerful and gives us a cutting capacity of up to 2-1/2 inch thick steel plate. We specialize in cutting custom anchor bolt templates and heavy-duty anchor plate washers made to order for your construction projects. Beyond structural hardware, we can also cut out any custom shapes or parts our customers need from sheet metal.
